GPOTS moves to a new university every year, and each host builds its own website
for that year. Those sites are the authority on registration, the program, and
local arrangements. They also tend to disappear once the meeting is over.

This site is the permanent address. It points at the current meeting, keeps a
record of past ones, and carries the announcement list.

## Organizing an upcoming meeting

Send your dates, location, and website link to
[webmaster@gpots.org](mailto:webmaster@gpots.org) and they will be added here.
Nothing on this site updates on its own, so please do write.

## Who maintains it

This site is maintained on behalf of the GPOTS community. The current webmaster
is José Carrión.

Questions about these pages, and corrections to the record of past meetings, go
to [webmaster@gpots.org](mailto:webmaster@gpots.org). Questions about the
conference itself go to
[organizers@gpots.org](mailto:organizers@gpots.org), which reaches whoever is
organizing the next meeting.

## Source

The site is a small set of plain text files kept at
[github.com/gpots-info](https://github.com/gpots-info/gpots-info.github.io).
Corrections are welcome there or by email.
