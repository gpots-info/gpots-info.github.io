GPOTS moves to a new university every year, and each host builds its own website
for that year.

This site only points at the current meeting, keeps a
record of past ones, and carries the announcement list.

## Organizing an upcoming meeting

Send your dates, location, and website link to
[webmaster@gpots.org](mailto:webmaster@gpots.org) and they will be added here.

## Who maintains it

This site is maintained on behalf of the GPOTS community. The current webmaster is José Carrión.

Questions about these pages, and corrections to the record of past meetings, go
to [webmaster@gpots.org](mailto:webmaster@gpots.org). Questions about the
conference itself go to
[organizers@gpots.org](mailto:organizers@gpots.org), which reaches whoever is
organizing the next meeting.
